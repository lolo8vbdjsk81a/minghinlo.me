Privacy Policy

Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This app is a school project developed by VisualAL as part of the course CCIT4080-CL07/11 (Project on Knowledge Products Development) at HKU SPACE (University of Hong Kong School of Professional and Continuing Education) during the 2024/2025 academic year. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information Collection

We may collect IP addresses to help us maintain server functionality and monitor usage patterns. However, we do not associate these IP addresses with any personal information, such as names or emails.

Use of Information

The information we collect is used to improve our services, communicate updates, and conduct anonymous surveys.

Anonymous Surveys

Our surveys are designed to be anonymous. We do not collect any identifiable information from users participating in surveys, ensuring that your responses remain private.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with third-party services for analytics purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access and disclosure.

User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your information at any time. You can also withdraw consent for data processing.

Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time and will notify you of any changes through the app.

Contact Information

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our contact form.
